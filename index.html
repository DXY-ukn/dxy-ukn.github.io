Xiangyu Ding

About me

I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at the Center for Combinatorics, Nankai University, under the supervision of Professor Lisa Hui Sun. I joined the integrated graduate-to-PhD programme in 2021, entered the doctoral stage in 2023, and expect to complete my PhD in June 2027.

My research interests include integer partitions, q-series, Bailey methods, truncated theta series, and Rogers–Ramanujan-type identities.
