Research

Partitions and truncated theta series. My joint work uses Bailey lattices, partition bijections and the circle method to study truncated series. We proved bivariate nonnegativity of normalized Jacobi triple product tails and Merca’s stronger conjecture. Related work develops conjugate WP–Bailey transformations and regular-partition inequalities.

Bailey theory and CMPP identities. With Lisa Hui Sun, we developed symmetrized B2 Bailey chains and lattices. A two-base construction with Weyl-orbit decompositions proves four conjectured D3(2) multisum identities and expands the products in the n = 2 case of Primc’s D conjecture.

PBW bases and affine characters. I used ordered PBW bases, tensor products and annihilating-field relations to establish the proof of Cn(1) type CMPP partition conjecture for arbitrary boundary.
