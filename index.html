Research

Truncated theta series. My joint work with Lisa Hui Sun uses the Bailey lattice to obtain Andrews–Gordon-type truncations and new Euler and Gauss specializations. Our paper accepted by JCTA proves bivariate coefficientwise nonnegativity for normalized Jacobi triple product tails, establishing Merca’s stronger conjecture through a sign-reversing involution, generalized minimal-excludant classes and Frobenius-arm injections.

Partition methods. I study explicit partition bijections based on Durfee rectangles, staircase operations and Gaussian refactorization, together with conjugate WP–Bailey transformations. My analytic work gives effective eventual-nonnegativity thresholds through the circle method and restricted-partition estimates. Further joint work derives regular-partition inequalities from exact counts of partitions into parts 1, 2 and 4.

Bailey methods and character identities. Our symmetrized B2 framework develops finite kernel identities, Bailey chains and lattices, and two families of roots. Their iterations yield Rogers–Ramanujan-type multisums, including expressions for selected principally specialized affine A1 characters and extremal CMPP products.
